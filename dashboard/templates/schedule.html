When each board is scanned

Enabled is boards.<name>.enabled in config.yml: whether the board runs at all, for the timer, the terminal and “scan all” alike. Scheduled is this timetable: when the timer should start it. A board that is scheduled but not enabled never runs.
The timer asks every ten minutes whether anything is due, and a scan you start yourself counts — a board you have just scanned is not scanned again at its next slot.
